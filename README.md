AI-Based Resume and Interview Platform

 Project Overview

The AI-Based Resume and Interview Platform is an AI-powered system designed to help users improve their resumes and prepare for job interviews.

The platform uses Artificial Intelligence to analyze resumes, provide useful feedback, and support users in their interview preparation.

 Objectives

- Analyze and improve resumes using AI.
- Help users identify strengths and areas for improvement in their resumes.
- Provide interview preparation support.
- Help users improve their confidence and readiness for job interviews.
- Provide a user-friendly platform for career preparation.

 Key Features

-  Resume Analysis – Analyze resume content and provide feedback.
- AI-Based Suggestions – Generate suggestions to improve resume quality.
-  Interview Preparation – Help users prepare for interview questions.
- Performance Feedback – Provide feedback to help users improve.
-  Career Support – Assist users in preparing for job opportunities.


Technologies

This project uses Artificial Intelligence and related software technologies to support resume analysis and interview preparation.

Future Enhancements

- Real-time AI mock interviews.
- Voice-based interview practice.
- Advanced resume scoring.
- Personalized job recommendations.
- Integration with job portals.
- Detailed interview performance analytics.
